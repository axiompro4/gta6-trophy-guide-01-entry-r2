      ⚠ The one trophy you can miss

      Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 meditations at 10 locations). The first meditation is Lucia's on the balcony at Jason's house in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth: she talks about meditating in the kitchen in the morning, then walks to the balcony. Follow her and accept at once — it never returns after Chapter 1. Whether it counts toward the trophy is unconfirmed, so do it to be safe. Sources: PowerPyx (All Meditation Spot Locations), GameMapa, GameTrek.

      Five trophies are still unconfirmed

      In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin have no confirmed method yet (PowerPyx via eGamers). This guide flags them honestly and gives the best current lead for each — check updated guides before grinding them.

      Rules that cost you trophies

      • Cheats disable trophies and saving (GTABase, TechWiser via eGamers) — leave cheats off for the whole run; the only fix is a clean save.
      • No mission replay. Keep a spread of manual saves as you go, especially before Chapter 1 ends.
      • Stranger chains have windows. One source (TechWiser via eGamers) reports the stranger mission chains behind five hidden trophies have windows tied to story progress — begin each chain as soon as it unlocks.
      • Avoid the Irredeemable criminal profile — it is permanent and makes the police more aggressive (PowerPyx via eGamers).
      • Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable: everything can still be done in free roam after the story (PowerPyx).
